MakeoverMonday - Global GDP Rankings Redesign

Step 1: Original Visualization

Original Visualization: Global GDP Ranks - Statistics of the World

I chose this visualization because I am interested in the global economy and the GDP differences between countries. The original visualization shows the GDP rankings, values, and percentages of world GDP in a table.

I think this is an interesting topic because it allows viewers to understand which countries have the largest economies. However, I wanted to see if a different chart type could make the GDP differences easier to compare.

Step 2: Critique

I used Stephen Few's Data Visualization Effectiveness Profile to evaluate the original visualization.

The original table clearly shows the GDP rankings and specific values. However, it is difficult to compare GDP differences directly because it mainly shows numbers. I think a bar chart could make the comparison more clear and visually engaging.

Step 3: Sketch a Solution

My initial idea was to use a horizontal bar chart to show the GDP rankings. I wanted to arrange countries from highest to lowest GDP and include the values next to each bar.
